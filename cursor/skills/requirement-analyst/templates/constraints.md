# Constraints

Limits and rules inferred from architecture diagrams, design documents, and source artifacts. Constraints are not requirements; they bound what solutions may do.

Keep each constraint atomic. If a constraint overlaps a requirement, policy, or invariant, keep the strongest canonical item and reference its ID instead of restating it.
